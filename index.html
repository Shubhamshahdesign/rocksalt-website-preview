About us

A wee café with a big counter

Rocksalt sits just off the Royal Mile, on the street that links Waverley Station to the Canongate. It is the kind of place locals use for a quick coffee and visitors are glad they found.

The counter is the heart of it. Fresh salads, meze and Mediterranean dishes sit next to Scottish favourites like haggis, potato scone and black pudding. Our borek is made with filo toasted three times, and the cakes and baklava are on the counter from first thing.

Order and pay at the counter, take a seat, and we will bring it over.

  • Breakfast & brunch Full Scottish, French toast, pancakes, menemen and avocado toast.
  • Lunch Salads, platters, burgers, rolls, paninis and jacket potatoes.
  • Deli & bakes Borek, baklava, tray bakes and cakes to take away.
